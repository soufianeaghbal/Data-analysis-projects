# factors associated with Students academic performance

In this project i analyzed 1,000 students' exam scores, trying to examine which factors really matters to students performance are they the direct factors which is basically preparing for exams or is there some indirect variables that affects students performance such as their parents education levels, gender and more variables 

Tools : MySQL, Tableau, Excel 

## 1. Research question and objectives 

### Research Question 
To what extent is students' academic performance (math, reading, writing and there overall performance) associated with their exam preparation and the other indirect factors (parent education, lunch type, ethnicity, and gender) ? 

### Objectives 
1. examine the relationship between exam prepartion and students academic performance.
2. examine indirect factors effect on students academic performance.
3. indenfity which indirect factors whos the strongest association wiht academic performance.
4. Compare the association of the direct factor and the indirect factors .

### Hypotheses 
### Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Tested in |
|---|---|
| Students who completed the test preparation course score higher in all subjects than those who did not. | Task 2 |
| Students whose parents have higher education levels score higher than students whose parents have lower education levels. | Task 3 |
| Students with standard lunch score higher than students with free/reduced lunch. | Task 3 |
| Average scores differ across ethnic groups and between genders. | Task 3 |
| Among the indirect factors, lunch type and parental education show the largest score gaps. | Task 4 |
| The score gap associated with exam preparation is smaller than the gaps associated with the strongest indirect factors. | Task 5 |
